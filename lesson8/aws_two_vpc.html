Lesson 8 · variant · two VPCs · ap-northeast-1

Two teams, two VPCs, one private service link

1 · Reachability matrix

Compared with the single-VPC design, order no longer shares a network with payment. It can reach one port through PrivateLink, and nothing at all comes back the other way.

2 · The cross-account call

Payment never sees a 10.10.x.x address. PrivateLink hands it NLB traffic, so neither team's CIDRs, routes or NACLs depend on the other's.
